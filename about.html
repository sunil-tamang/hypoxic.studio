From Nepal.
Beyond the familiar.

An independent filmmaking studio shaped by altitude, curiosity, and the desire to document what few people get to witness.

It started with a dream
to reach the summit.

Before Hypoxic Studio had a name, we shared an ambition: to reach the summit of an 8,000-metre mountain with our cameras. We were fascinated by what happens at those altitudes — and by how little real footage existed of what it truly feels like to be there.

We wanted to take our cameras into those environments, document what unfolds beyond the familiar, and bring back perspectives that few people ever get to witness.

That curiosity became the foundation of Hypoxic Studio. What began with high-altitude filmmaking has grown into a wider vision: telling stories of adventure, people, places, and experiences that deserve to be seen and heard.

/ Our approach 02

  1. 01

    Research & discovery

    We listen to your goals, understand the audience, and research the people and places at the heart of the story.

  2. 02

    Creative direction

    We develop the idea, visual approach, and narrative together, shaping a clear direction before the shoot.

  3. 03

    Pre-production

    We plan the crew, equipment, locations, and logistics around the brief, with room to adapt to changing conditions.

  4. 04

    Production

    We capture the story with care, staying attentive to the people, environment, and unexpected moments that make it real.

  5. 05

    Post-production & delivery

    We bring the film together through editing, colour, and sound, refine it with your feedback, and prepare the final deliverables.
