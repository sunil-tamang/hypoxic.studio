/ FAQ

Questions?
We’ve got you.

Here are some common questions about working with Hypoxic Studio. If you don’t find what you’re looking for, feel free to reach out.

01

What types of projects do you take on?

We work on documentary films, brand stories, expeditions, and creative projects with purpose.

02

Where are you based and do you work internationally?

We’re based in Kathmandu, Nepal, and work with clients and partners worldwide.

03

What is the typical process for a project?

We start with a conversation, define the scope, plan logistics, and move into production.

04

Do you work with brands and agencies?

Yes. We collaborate with brands, agencies and organisations on creative and documentary work.

05

Can you help with permits and logistics in Nepal?

Tell us where you plan to film. We can discuss permit requirements, local logistics, and the support your project needs.

06

How far in advance should we get in touch?

Ideally 2–3 months in advance, especially for expeditions or remote locations. Get in touch to discuss your timeline.

07

Do you take on smaller projects or content pieces?

Yes. We also take on smaller projects, including short films, photo stories and content pieces.

08

How can we collaborate or partner with you?

Share a bit about your idea, goals, and timeline, and we’ll take it from there.
